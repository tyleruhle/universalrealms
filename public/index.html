What is Universal Realms
The Vision
Universal Realms sits between a tabletop rulebook, an AI world generator, and a strategy game. Every tool feeds into the same living world — characters are born into factions, factions wage wars, wars reshape maps, maps define the world your next character inhabits.
Built for Game Masters
Designed for GMs running persistent campaigns, players building characters with depth, and creators who want a world that feels like history rather than a static backdrop. Every system is modular — use what you need, ignore what you don't.
Any Genre, Any Era
Starting in 1066 Europe but built to support any setting — high fantasy, ancient mythology, political intrigue, post-war reconstruction, or worlds that don't exist yet. The tools adapt to your world, not the other way around.
Current Status
Version 0.1 Alpha. Five tools are live and functional. The full platform — AI world generation, persistent saves, user accounts, and multiplayer — is in active development. Feedback and playtesting welcome.
